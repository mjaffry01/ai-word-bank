Privacy

AI Concepts course site

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the site receives your name and email address from Google. When you finish a ring quiz, it saves the date, the ring, your score, and the answer you chose for each question.

Why

Your teacher uses your email to approve you for the course, and uses your scores and answers to follow your progress. Nothing else.

Where it is kept

In a private Google Sheet that only your teacher can open. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing your data

Ask your teacher, and they will delete your rows from the Sheet.
