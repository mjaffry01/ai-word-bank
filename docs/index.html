AI Concepts

The vocabulary of artificial intelligence as a learning web. The core words sit in the centre, and each ring outward holds words that build on the rings inside it. Teach from the centre out, ring by ring.

Put the vocabulary to work: write prompts that get good answers, use the main prompting techniques, and catch AI when it makes things up.

Look inside a chatbot: see text split into real tokens, pick the next word with a temperature dial, watch attention find meaning, and see why models hallucinate and forget.

See what happens before an AI ever answers: how data becomes a model, train your own spam filter, watch a model overfit, and train an image model with your webcam.
